# Topical Authority — epoxylantai.co.id

## Role and boundary

`epoxylantai.co.id` is an independent Indonesian specialist publication and commercial-support property for resin flooring, centred on epoxy floor systems from substrate survey through renewal. Its useful audience is facility owners, operators, HSE and QA staff, consultants, quantity surveyors, procurement teams, and applicators who need to turn operating conditions into a defensible floor-system decision.

The editorial scope includes concrete-substrate condition, moisture, resin-system selection, preparation, details and joints, application, inspection, diagnosis, maintenance, repair, safety, performance claims, and procurement. `/cat-epoxy/`, `/epoxy-lantai/`, `/epoxy-pu-coating/`, `/epoxy-pu-concrete/`, and legitimate `/pasang/*` pages own commercial service or product intent; knowledge articles must not become disguised quote pages. General concrete casting, injection repair, waterproofing, and dry-shake floor hardener remain supporting substrate interfaces rather than equal editorial pillars.

This domain may independently cover subjects also discussed by `epoxy.co.id`, `kontraktorepoxy.co.id`, and `lantai.id`. Cross-domain similarity is not cannibalization. Within this domain, every planned page has one primary job, an explicit exclusion, and a named owner for the excluded intent.

Indonesia is the geographic context, but the plan creates no city-swapped articles or subdomains. A local page is justified only by genuinely different climate, regulation, supply, building operation, or documented project evidence.

- [ISO 2812-1:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/71584.html) and [ISO 2812-2:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/71585.html) cover coating resistance to non-water liquids and water immersion.

- [IEC 61340-5-1:2024](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/74748) governs an ESD-control programme for handling susceptible electronics. A generic “antistatic” product label is not equivalent to a verified installed system.
- [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene revision](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/codex-alimentarius-commission-21-25-november-2022/en) is a food-hygiene/HACCP framework, not a flooring product certificate.
- [OSHA crystalline-silica construction guidance](https://www.osha.gov/silica-crystalline/construction) documents the need for exposure controls during dust-generating concrete work. It is technical evidence, not Indonesian law.
- [HSE chemical guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/chindex.htm) identifies dermatitis and isocyanate risks and prioritizes substance assessment and engineering controls. Product-specific SDS and the actual application method govern the job.
- [Permenakertrans PER.08/MEN/VII/2010](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/asset/data_puu/peraturan_file_PER08.pdf) is an Indonesian primary source for PPE duties; current applicability and project-specific legal interpretation require an Indonesian K3 reviewer.
- [ISO 16000-9:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/79022.html) is a chamber method for VOC emissions from building-product samples. “Low VOC” content, emission rate, odour, and safe re-entry are different claims.

## Existing coverage and risks

The site has broad URL volume but very little demonstrated editorial depth. Most indexed candidates differ by place name while retaining the same service intent. This creates same-domain duplication, crawl dilution, weak evidence alignment, and misleading impressions of local presence. The authority plan does not count those pages as topic coverage.

The eight service families also blur important distinctions: epoxy resin, PU coating, polyurethane-cement systems, dry-shake hardener, concrete repair, and waterproofing solve different problems. Existing superlatives such as “#1”, “terbaik”, and “murah” require substantiation or removal. Product resistance, thickness, cure, slip, fire, food-hygiene, ESD, VOC, and warranty statements must be tied to an identified system, substrate, test method, conditions, and current documents.

The video sitemap reuses nine videos across 1,479 entries. Until each route has page-specific footage, provenance, and consent, the video cannot support a city, facility, performance, or completed-project claim. Legacy `.html`, no-slash, slash, and `-2` variants need a measured URL decision based on Search Console, backlinks, leads, and live response behavior before redirects or removals.

## Evidence and editorial standards

1. **Claim ladder:** sourced explanation < current manufacturer TDS/SDS < laboratory report for the named system/build-up < project-specific field test < documented outcome. State which rung supports each material claim.
2. **Qualified gates:** substrate/structural advice needs a competent concrete/material specialist; K3 and legal statements need an Indonesian K3/legal reviewer; ventilation or exposure claims need an occupational-hygiene/ventilation professional; fire, slip, food-hygiene, and ESD compliance claims need the relevant designer/test laboratory/authority.
3. **Current documents:** record manufacturer, exact product/system, document title, revision/date, source URL/file, substrate, layers, thickness, conditioning, test method, result, and limitations. Recheck on every substantive update.
4. **No borrowed thresholds:** moisture, profile, adhesion, thickness, cure/recoat, temperature, humidity, slip, ESD, fire, chemical resistance, VOC, and release-to-service values come only from a current project specification, applicable standard, or named-system document. Do not generalize a value from one product.
5. **Safety hierarchy:** eliminate/substitute where feasible, isolate/engineer controls, administrative controls, then task- and substance-compatible PPE. A glove or respirator type is not specified without SDS, exposure assessment, compatibility, fit, and competent review.
6. **Case-study integrity:** require project permission, date, facility context, substrate, system identity, area source, method, QC records, photographs with provenance, deviations, and measured outcome. Otherwise label content a hypothetical worked example.
7. **Images and video:** original or licensed, with subject, location level appropriate to consent, date, and relationship to the claim. Reused generic footage cannot prove a specific city or project.
8. **Commercial transparency:** distinguish education, manufacturer data, quoted scope, and operator experience. Disclose assumptions and exclusions; do not use “#1”, “best”, “food grade”, “non-slip”, “fireproof”, “antibacterial”, “zero VOC”, or “lifetime” without defined comparative evidence.
9. **Update cadence:** safety, legal, standards, and manufacturer claims at publication and at least annually; other evergreen pages every 18–24 months or when failures/questions reveal a gap.
