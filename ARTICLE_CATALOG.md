# Article Catalog — epoxylantai.co.id

Scope: 84 evidence-led article briefs for an Indonesian resin-flooring specialist, covering the complete decision and lifecycle path from substrate survey to renewal.

Domain boundaries: articles support but do not replace the commercial routes `/epoxy-lantai/`, `/cat-epoxy/`, `/epoxy-pu-coating/`, `/epoxy-pu-concrete/`, `/pasang/*`, and `/kontak-kami/`. General concrete, waterproofing, injection, and floor-hardener services appear only where they affect a resin-floor decision. Topics may overlap independently with `epoxy.co.id`, `kontraktorepoxy.co.id`, and `lantai.id`; only same-domain intent overlap is controlled. No city-swapped brief is permitted.

Catalog statistics: 14 parent topics; 84 briefs; 6 briefs per topic; 12 P0, 40 P1, 32 P2, 0 P3. Wave 1 has 12 assets; later waves are bounded by evidence readiness and measured demand.

Priority legend: `P0` = first linked cluster or critical risk/decision asset; `P1` = next high-value lifecycle coverage; `P2` = depth after evidence and demand; `P3` = optional/experimental. Waves: `W1-foundation`, `W2-delivery`, `W3-operations`, `W4-specialist`.
